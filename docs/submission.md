# Formula, a RealGame world: Game Week submission

**Category:** Option 1, Exploration / Adventure
**Play:** (link added after deploy) — runs in the browser, nothing to install. Best in Chrome or Safari on a laptop from the last few years.

## The pitch

The Great Kiln that warmed the valley and made all its glass has gone cold. You are a young alchemist whose spells are real chemistry. Learn formulas at the Formula Bench, practise them until they become powers, and use them to cross a frozen river, smother a burning marsh, light a dark crystal cave, and invent a reaction hot enough to relight the Kiln.

Every spell runs through a real simulation of heat, phase changes, fire, water and gases. Water puts out fire because it cools fuel below its ignition point. Frost freezes water into ice you can walk on. Carbon dioxide is heavier than air, so it pools in hollows and smothers flames. A hydrogen balloon lifts because hydrogen is lighter than air.

## How it plays (10 to 15 minutes)

1. **The Village:** learn Water at the Formula Bench and put out the burning workshop.
2. **The Frozen Fields:** practise Water to open the gate, then freeze the ford with Frost to cross it. Melt the ice gate with Fire.
3. **The Marsh:** block the gap in a methane hollow, then pour in CO2 to smother all six vents.
4. **The Crystal Cave:** make a torch, split water into hydrogen and oxygen at the electrolysis rig, and pick the gas that lifts the crate to the Kiln Spark.
5. **The Kiln Terrace:** open the Kiln door with the Spark, then invent a reaction at the Invention Bench that burns hotter than 1700 °C.

As you learn and practise, your rank grows from Spark to Archon. Each spell also becomes a power: Flare, an H2 lift, an ice walkway and a CO2 dash. Five hidden caches reward exploring with the right chemistry.

## Made for this game

Every asset is generated by our code when the game runs. Nothing is pre-made or taken from a library.
- **World and props:** the terrain is a voxel simulation drawn as a smooth surface. Trees, crystals, cliffs, buildings and the mountains are built procedurally, and materials and the sky are shaders written for the game.
- **Sound effects:** more than 40, all synthesised in the browser with the Web Audio API.
- **Music:** a theme for each area plus an ending sting, composed and synthesised in code.
- **Interface:** HTML and CSS, with icons drawn as inline SVG.

`node tests/asset-audit.mjs` checks the shipped build: it fails on any image, model, audio, video or font file, any asset loaded from another site, and any media compiled into the binary.

## Built with

Rust and Bevy compiled to WebAssembly, with TypeScript and JavaScript for the interface. It is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, with anonymous analytics (age band only, no personal data). Every science claim in the game is listed with its source in `content/science-sources.md`.
