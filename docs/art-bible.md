# Formula art bible

One page that every visual in Formula is checked against. A new asset is done only when a separate critic has compared it with this page and with an in-game capture at play scale (the lecture's creator, critic, in-game check loop; see `/mnt/project-files/realgame/research/game-assets-lecture.md`).

## Direction in one line

A warm, sunlit valley of soft sculpted ground and simple faceted props, where science shows up as cool teal light: a sci-fi world where cultivation stories come true with tech that could be real.

## Style

- **Family:** stylized 3D. Rounded, readable silhouettes; simplified materials; no photo textures, no pixel tiles, no hard voxel edges.
- **Ground:** smooth Surface Nets terrain with soft value-noise variation. Grass on flats and rock on slopes, blended by slope. Cliffs have ridged tops, buttresses and scree.
- **Props (trees, rocks, crystals):** low-poly and faceted, flat-shaded per facet, with 6 to 12 sides per silhouette. Faceted props on smooth ground is the deliberate contrast.
- **Built things (houses, workshop, Kiln, Benches, gates, rig):** rounded and bevelled, never sharp boxes. Clay, timber and stone, with chunky proportions (doors about 1.4 times player height, thick walls).
- **Science and magic:** the only cool, glowing element. Teal rune bands, crystal glow, spell light and Bench screens. Glow is earned: anything that is not science stays non-emissive.

## Light

- **Day areas:** warm sun (about 5200 K) from high side-front, soft blue sky fill, a faint rim on silhouettes. Shadows are soft and readable.
- **Frozen Fields:** cooler and brighter, with low sun and long shadows.
- **Marsh:** hazy, with lower contrast and yellow-green fog near the ground.
- **Crystal Cave:** dark until the torch. Then warm torch light near the player and teal crystal glow as the only other light.
- **Kiln:** the hearth's orange glow drives the finale; when the Kiln is relit, the valley warms.
- Emissive values stay under the bloom ceiling. Glow should read as light, not white blobs.

## Scale and level of detail

- The player is 1.8 m; 1 world block is 1 m.
- Props near the path carry the most detail. Beyond 60 m, props drop to simpler meshes and the backdrop is silhouettes.
- Readability first: a 6-year-old must tell the path, the objective and an interactable apart at a glance.

## UI

- Rounded dark-navy panels, white text, the yellow highlight for the current item, and teal for science values.
- One objective line top-left; prompts appear at the bottom centre; nothing covers the crosshair.
- Formulas use real subscripts (CO₂, H₂O).

## Critic checklist (per asset or shot)

- Matches the palette and family above; no stray cube edges.
- Silhouette reads at play distance.
- Glow is only on science and magic, and not blown out.
- Lighting fits the area.
- Nothing pre-made: the asset is generated by our code.
