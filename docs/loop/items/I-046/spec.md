Written at b8fa548 and refreshed in wave 11 (see "Refresh (wave 11)" at the end); every line number below is at e70df08 unless it says "at b8fa548". **Track:** product. **Bars:** B4 (no persona loses the fire while casting), B1 (golden stays green, plus new checks). **Source:** `docs/loop/items/I-025/break-1.md` Should-fix 1 (close-up slab) and Should-fix 2 (the golden check that ties "casts once" to "fire out"), plus `docs/loop/items/I-044/spec.md` Out of scope follow-up "stream starts at the lifted hand" (I-044 break-2). `main.rs` means `crates/bevy-client/src/main.rs`, `feel.rs` means `crates/bevy-client/src/feel.rs`, `cues.rs` means `crates/bevy-client/src/cues.rs`, `golden` means `tests/e2e/golden.mjs`.

## Problem (measured at b8fa548; the stream code is unchanged at e70df08)

- `cues::stream_style` (`cues.rs:232-271`) gives every stream `thick: 0.22`, and Water, Frost and CO2 get an inverted-hull outline at `outline_scale: 1.3`, so the rod is 0.286 m across. `stream_transform` (`main.rs:252-261`) scales the shared `kit.cyl` to that width along the whole stream, from the hand to the head. `spawn_stream` (`main.rs:267-306`) starts it at the hand: camera space `(0.2, -0.15, -0.5)` (`main.rs:1208`, in `aim_and_cast`).
- **Geometry.** With `f` the focal length in px (View width held, so `f = VW/2` at the default 90°), a stream to a target on the view axis passes through screen points at radius `r = 0.25·f/z` from the crosshair, toward the hand's screen point `Hs = C + (0.4f, 0.3f)`. A stream `T` m across is `T·f/z = 4T·r` px wide there. So the far part is a wedge from the crosshair with half-angle `atan(2T)` around the direction 37° below the rightward horizontal. At `T = 0.286` that is ±30°, so the wedge's top edge is about 7° below horizontal. The near end at the hand is `T/0.5 = 0.57 f` wide (about 33° of view) and ends in a disc about 0.29 f in radius around `Hs`.
- **What players see.** In `/tmp/claude-0/brk9-play-shots/kid7-21x9-3-water-1.png` (1680×720) and `kid7-4x3-3-frost-1.png` (1024×768), the blue or white slab with its navy or slate outline fills the lower right quadrant from the crosshair to the screen edge, under the "Aim at a fire" hint, with the gauntlet poking out of it. A sphere-swept model of the rod (scratch script, reproducible from the numbers above) gives frame coverage of 14 % at 21:9, 10 % at 4:3 and 13 % at 16:9. A fire 0.20·VH right of the crosshair at crosshair height is about 45 % hidden. One 0.30·VH right and 10° lower is 97-100 % hidden. I-025's GS walk estimated 6 %, because it left out the near-end disc and the outline.
- **Constraint from I-025.** A visible gap between the hand and the stream was rejected (I-025 GS walk, row 6). I-019's first-frame blue-pixel check and GS-12 depend on the stream's near end. I-025 test 7 needs a visible run of at least `0.6 r` at `r = 20..110` px (depths 8 m to 1.45 m, which is 0.95 m or more from the hand). I-025's unit test pins `thick` to [0.18, 0.25] and `outline_scale` to [1.15, 1.35].

## Decision: taper the first 0.8 m, and use the thin end of I-025's range

1. **Taper.** The stream's width at distance `s` m from the hand is `w(s) = thick · clamp(s / lead, lead_min, 1)`, with `lead = 0.8` m and `lead_min = 0.15`. At the hand it is about 3 cm across, about the width of a finger, and it touches the hand, so there is no gap. From 0.8 m on it is the full width, which covers everything I-025 test 7 measures (0.95 m and beyond). The widest it can look anywhere is `thick·outline / (0.5 + lead)` rad, down from 0.57 f to 0.17 f. This applies to all four spells. Fire has no outline, but it has the same near-end slab.
2. **Thickness.** `thick` goes from 0.22 to **0.18** and `outline_scale` from 1.3 to **1.2** for Water, Frost and CO2. Fire's `thick` also goes to 0.18 (Fire is exempt from U1's 0.1875 lower bound, see U1). Both values are inside I-025's unit-test limits, so that test is not edited. The rod becomes 0.216 m across, the wedge's half-angle drops to 23°, and its top edge moves to about 14° below horizontal. Test 7's run at `r` becomes about `0.86 r` against the `0.6 r` it needs (it measured `1.25 r` at 0.286 m).
3. **Why both.** In the model, the taper alone fixes 4:3 but leaves a fire right of the crosshair 40 % hidden at 21:9, and the band right of the crosshair 22 % covered. Thinning alone (0.18 × 1.15) still leaves a near end 0.41 f wide. Together they give frame coverage of 2.9 % at 21:9 and 1.7 % at 4:3, the band 9 % and 2.5 %, and fire visibility 93-100 %. Rejected options: a lead-in gap (I-025); one frustum over the whole stream, which is too thin at 1-7 m, gives 0.41 r at r = 110 and fails test 7; a per-cast mesh asset, which churns assets every cast (B2).
4. **Mesh, smallest form.** One new shared mesh, a `ConicalFrustum` (radius 0.5 at the far end, `0.5·lead_min` at the near end, height 1, resolution 10, axis +Y like `kit.cyl`), built once in `setup` and held in a new field of `SpellMats` (so `spawn_stream`'s signature is unchanged). Each stream draws two parts. The **lead** is the frustum from the hand to `min(len, lead)`, scaled `(thick·k, lead_len, thick·k)` with `k = clamp(len/lead, lead_min, 1)`. The **rod** is today's `kit.cyl` from `lead` to the head, only when `len > lead`. Each part gets the same outline child rule as today. Suggested structure: the `Stream` entity becomes an unmeshed root (identity transform, `Visibility`) with the two parts as children, and stores their `Entity` ids so `projectiles` can set their transforms. The builder may pick another structure if these invariants hold: `streams` still counts one entity per live cast; every part and outline despawns with its `Stream` (impact plus `STREAM_AFTER`, a reset, the epoch); parts freeze while paused; mist (`main.rs:1289-1313`) still uses `from` and `head`; `kit` meshes are never mutated.
5. **Pure math in `cues.rs`.** `StreamStyle` gains `lead: f32` and `lead_min: f32`. New `pub fn stream_width(s: f32, style: &StreamStyle) -> f32` and `pub fn stream_split(len: f32, style: &StreamStyle) -> (f32 /*lead_len*/, f32 /*lead_scale k*/, f32 /*rod_len*/)`, Bevy-free, used by `main.rs` to place the parts.

6. **The stream starts at the turned hand (I-044 follow-up).** Since I-044, the gauntlet's rest pose is `feel::VM_REST` turned about the eye by `Rx(feel::vm_tilt_deg(settings.fov_y()))` (`main.rs:646-651` in `setup`, `main.rs:1644-1648` in `viewmodel`). The hand point is not turned, so at 1680×720 with View width 70 (vfov 33.4°, tilt 5.9°) the stream starts 16.7° below centre, at the bottom edge, about 5° below the gem (11.7°). Fix: the hand becomes `eye + player.rotation() * Rx(tilt) * (0.2, -0.15, -0.5)`, with the same `settings.fov_y()` the gauntlet uses (not `aim_and_cast`'s local `fov_y` at `main.rs:1054`, which reads the window aspect). The math is one new pure function appended to `feel.rs` after `vm_rest` (`feel.rs:144-150`): `pub const VM_HAND: [f32; 3] = [0.2, -0.15, -0.5];` and `pub fn vm_hand(vfov_deg: f32) -> [f32; 3]`, which returns `Rx(vm_tilt_deg(vfov_deg))·VM_HAND` and exactly `VM_HAND` when the tilt is 0. `main.rs:1208` calls it. The one `hand` value still feeds both `spawn_stream` and the projectile ball (`main.rs:1228-1243`), so the ball and the stream keep a shared start, and `dist` (flight time) changes by under 1 %. Only the rest-pose turn is followed: bob, recoil and sway are not (as before). At every view where the tilt is 0 (16:9 and 4:3 at View width 90, 21:9 at View width 90; see `feel.rs` tests) nothing changes, so G1-G4 are unaffected.

Unchanged: the stream origin at the default view (`VM_HAND`, tilt 0), colours, `STREAM_CORE_GAIN`, flight times, the projectile ball, mist, the viewmodel, aim and assist.
